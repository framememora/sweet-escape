Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

This website doesn't have accounts, a checkout, or a contact form that saves anything. We don't sell or share your information. When you order, you talk to us directly on WhatsApp or Instagram.

Who we are

The Sweet Escape is a dessert shop with counters in Thoppumpady and Fort Kochi, Kerala, India. You can reach us on WhatsApp or on Instagram at @the_sweet_escapenew_.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The "made to order" form on our home page runs entirely in your browser. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", it opens WhatsApp with your message filled in. Nothing is sent to us until you choose to send that message.

Services this site uses

  • Google Maps: the branch maps are embedded from Google, which may set cookies and collect usage data under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: our typefaces load from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram: messages you send us there are handled under Meta's privacy policies.
  • Hosting: our web host may keep standard server logs (like IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Messages you send us

When you message us about an order, we use your name, number and order details only to make and hand over your order, and to reply to you. We don't add you to marketing lists unless you ask.

Your choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what information we hold about you from your messages, and ask us to correct or delete it. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram and we'll sort it out.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
